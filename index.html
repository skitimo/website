About

Applied mathematics for data-rich scientific systems.

Sippanon Kitimoon is a computational applied mathematician with a Ph.D. in Mathematics from Claremont Graduate University and more than eight years of industry experience in software development, data analytics, and signal processing.

His work connects mathematical modeling, high-performance software, and data-driven methods for advanced analytics, engineering applications, recommender systems, and scientific computing. He joined the Data Science Research Center, Faculty of Science, Chiang Mai University on April 1, 2026.
